Our story

Started at 16.
Still hands-on.

Founded by Detroit native Juan Walker Jr., Sway Services was built on hard work, determination, and a commitment to quality service. Juan began his entrepreneurial journey at just 16 years old, growing his business from humble beginnings into a trusted provider of residential and commercial property services throughout Metro Detroit.

As a Michigan Licensed Residential Builder, Juan leads Sway Services with a hands-on approach, emphasizing professionalism, reliability, and customer satisfaction. With thousands of projects completed, Sway Services continues to expand its capabilities in property maintenance, construction, and exterior improvements.
